Welcome to My Website!

Hi! My name is Erika von Kirchbach, and I am currently a senior at Northeastern University studying Computer Science and Business Administration.

Outside of the classroom, I love expressing my creativity through cooking! To be honest, I hate following a recipe, so I began making my own! Through learning to cook with my mom, taking formal classes in high school, and even making it to Nationals in a cooking competition, I developed an expertise in cooking and feel very comfortable 'winging' my own recipes now. In this website, you will be able to find my favorite learned recipes, my favorite created recipes, and recipes I want to try!

As a student in college, I have started grocery shopping and meal planning on my own. However, I have run into a couple issues so far. First, my produce and other ingredients go bad before I am able to use it all up. For example, how am I single-handedly supposed to use a whole cabbage in a week before it rots in the back of my fridge? Also, I live with two other roommates, and we all share a fridge- meaning I get only one shelf for myself. This is the second issue: not enough storage.

These two issues led me to adopt themed cuisine weeks, to ensure I use all my ingredients before they go bad and clear out my shelf to have storage to buy more! This website explores these themed cuisine weeks and hopes to inspire others to do the same!

Please feel free to explore the Cuisines page to learn what a cuisine week looks like in practice!

Ready to plan your own? Try Meal Mate, the meal planning tool I built for students to solve these problems!
