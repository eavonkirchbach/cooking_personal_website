My Cuisine Weeks

Cooking for one in college comes with two big problems: ingredients go bad before I can use them up, and I only get one shelf in a fridge I share with two roommates. My solution is the cuisine week. Every week I pick one country, plan my breakfasts, lunches, and dinners around that cuisine, and buy a single shopping list of ingredients that shows up in almost every meal.

Because the meals share ingredients, nothing gets forgotten in the back of the fridge. The black beans I buy for Monday's breakfast burrito end up in Wednesday's burrito bowl, and the leftover steak from steak and vodka pasta becomes steak and eggs the next morning. By the end of the week my shelf is empty, and I have room to shop for the next cuisine.

Cuisine weeks also push me to keep learning. Some of the dishes below are recipes I learned from my mom or in cooking classes, some are my own creations from winging it with what was left in the fridge, and some are dishes I still want to try. Each recipe is tagged so you can tell which is which. Pick a country below to see what a full week of meals looks like, along with the shopping list that makes it work.
